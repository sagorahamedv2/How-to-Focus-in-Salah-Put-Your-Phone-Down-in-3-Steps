# STORYLINES: "How to Focus in Salah: Put Your Phone Down in 3 Steps"

Three different films for the same 195.6 s voiceover. All three are faceless, 1920×1080, 30 fps, and use live Arabic text in Amiri / Noto Naskh Arabic with full tashkeel. They differ in what the viewer is looking at: a world, a device, or a form.

Shared across all three:
- The ayah (23:1–2) and the du'a (14:40) are the two stillest moments. Recitation sits in the VO at about 25.6–30.9 s, so the music drops out and the picture slows there.
- Gold is reserved for the ayat and the three step words (Away, Silent, Replace). Nothing else is gold.
- The script says "four minutes" but the VO runs 3:16. The picture follows the VO.

---

## Option A: World ("The Quiet Path")

**Idea.** One continuous stylized home, seen in flat vector cutaway, like a dollhouse slice. The viewer's eye travels through it. The phone is a small glowing object that we follow from room to room, and each step of the film moves it somewhere quieter. The path to the prayer mat gets clearer as the film goes. This is the script's own last line made visual: "We're only clearing the path to it."

**Look.** Deep emerald night walls, warm sand floors and objects, a single soft lamp. Flat shapes, no outlines, subtle paper-cut shadow. A small minaret silhouette in the window of every room.

**Structure.**
1. Hook (0:00–0:12). Dim hallway. The phone lights up on a shelf with an adhan notification. A wall clock spins twenty minutes. A rushed prayer mat unrolls crooked.
2. Salam, promise, ayah (0:12–0:45). Camera pushes into the window, the room dissolves into a calm gold-on-emerald poster for the ayah.
3. The problem (0:45–1:08). Prayer mat in the middle of the room. Buzz rings expand from the phone and crack the calm. The three words land as three big objects in the same room.
4. Away (1:08–1:35). The phone slides through a door into another room (door-slide transition). A drawer closes on it. Cut to the office desk and locker for the Dhuhr example.
5. Silent (1:35–2:02). The phone on the nightstand. A 15-minute ring forms around the prayer time. Chat bubbles pile up outside the library window, then go grey and still.
6. Replace (2:02–2:25). An empty hand-shaped space on the mat turns into water for wudu, then a breath circle.
7. Dhuhr timeline, objection, start small (2:25–3:08). The house floor plan becomes the timeline. An alarm clock, a watch and a fridge timetable replace the phone's job. One of five prayer lamps stays lit.
8. CTA, du'a, salam (3:08–3:16). Camera pulls back to the whole house at dusk, one window lit. Du'a poster, then salam, then a 2 s hold.

**Hero moves.** Door-slide for Away, ring-around-the-phone for Silent, water-to-breath morph for Replace, pull-back to the lit window at the end.

**Strengths.** Most concrete. Every script image (drawer, locker, library, fridge) has a natural home. The hand-off object between scenes is the phone, so continuity is built in. Fits faceless with props and cutaways.

**Risks.** The most illustration to produce (about 15 SVG objects and several room sets). Needs a strict small kit of shapes so rooms stay consistent.

## Option B: Device ("Inside the Phone")

**Idea.** The phone is the protagonist and the screen is the stage. Most scenes happen on or around a phone UI: notification, lock screen, focus-mode settings, group chat, alarm. The three steps become three things you do to the device. At the end the phone goes dark and its black screen becomes the mihrab-shaped space where the prayer begins.

**Look.** Near-black teal with sand and gold. Crisp UI cards, rounded rects, real-feeling toggles and rings. Only slight stylization, so it reads as modern and familiar.

**Structure.**
1. Hook. Lock-screen notification, "Dhuhr adhan", a thumb-shaped tap shadow, then the feed. A timer overlay counts 20:00.
2. Promise and ayah. The screen dims. The ayah appears as a full-bleed poster that pushes the phone frame out of shot.
3. Problem. Notification stack builds on the screen, each one buzzing.
4. Away. The phone leaves the frame to the side, the screen shrinks to a point in a drawer.
5. Silent. Settings mock: focus mode toggle, 15-minute schedule ring, allowed-contacts filter. Group chat greys out.
6. Replace. The phone is gone. Only the leftover UI pieces (ring, toggle) turn into wudu drops and a breath circle.
7. Timeline, objection, start small. Timeline built from UI components. Alarm clock replaces the phone alarm app.
8. Close. Phone screen goes black, reflecting a mihrab arch, then du'a and salam.

**Hero moves.** Screen-to-poster push, toggle flip, schedule ring, black-screen-to-mihrab.

**Strengths.** Cheapest to build. UI is geometric, so it is easy to animate cleanly and seek-safely. Very clear for mobile viewers.

**Risks.** Feels like a tech explainer and may pull the tone away from a calm prayer film. Three of the steps (Away, Replace, objection) are about things that are not on the screen, so the device framing gets forced there. Risk of looking generic.

## Option C: Form ("Three Words")

**Idea.** A typographic and geometric film. No rooms and few literal objects. The three words Away, Silent and Replace are the whole language. Each step has its own formal system: Away is motion and distance, Silent is stillness and fading, Replace is one shape morphing into another. Islamic geometric pattern is the background texture, built slowly line by line.

**Look.** Emerald field, sand type, gold only for the ayat and the three words. Big Fraunces display type, Amiri Arabic, thin geometric linework, a slowly growing eight-fold star pattern.

**Structure.**
1. Hook. A single dot (the phone) pulses. Rings and a ticking arc show twenty minutes passing.
2. Promise and ayah. Pattern forms behind calligraphy.
3. Problem. The pattern fractures when the dot pulses, then repairs.
4. Away, Silent, Replace. Three formal chapters, each with a different layout: the dot exits the frame, a ring closes around the dot, the dot turns into a water drop and then a breath circle.
5. Examples. Minimal line icons (drawer, library, fridge) over the pattern.
6. Close. Pattern completes into a full star, du'a, salam.

**Hero moves.** Dot exit, closing ring, dot-to-drop morph, pattern completes.

**Strengths.** Most distinctive and most reverent. Fewest assets. Best for the ayat, du'a and salam moments.

**Risks.** Abstract. The script has several everyday scenes (office, library, fridge, family-only calls) that need literal pictures. Viewers may find 3+ minutes of type and shapes cold and lose interest without objects to attach to.

## Comparison

| | A: World | B: Device | C: Form |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Reads as | Warm story | Tech explainer | Reverent poster film |
| Matches script's concrete scenes | Best | Partial | Weakest |
| Asset load | High | Low | Lowest |
| Faceless fit | Good (props, cutaways) | Good | Best |
| Tone for ayat and du'a | Good | Weakest | Best |
| Production risk | Style drift across rooms | Looks generic | Feels cold or repetitive |

## Recommendation

**Option A (World).** The script is a sequence of concrete situations: adhan, another room, a drawer, a locker, a library, a fridge, a lit prayer path. A is the only option that shows all of them honestly, and it keeps the phone as a hand-off object from scene to scene, which the storyboard in task.md already assumes. To keep the cost down and the tone calm, borrow two things from the others: use C's quiet gold-on-emerald poster treatment for the ayah and du'a, and use B's clean UI mock only for the Silent scene, where a screen is the subject.
